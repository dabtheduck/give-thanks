🌱 Give Thanks — how it works

This is a counter with a memory. Press it when something good, small or ordinary happens.

COUNTING
• Open the app (the button below, or the Menu Button) and press "+ thanks" — once per thanks.
• Add a note if you like (up to 140 characters). Tap a note in Today's list to edit it later; × deletes that thanks.
• Or just type /thanks followed by a note right here in chat — it counts the same, and the app picks it up next time you open it.

YOUR DAY
• Your day runs until 3am. At 3am the counter resets and the notes disappear forever — unless you save them first.
• Save the list in your 1:1 chat with "Send today's recap to chat" in the app, or type /recap here.

SHARING
• Tick the share box on a note to make it shareable. Unticked notes stay private and show as blurred out.
• The Share tab in the app ("Share with friends") sends today's list outside Telegram, as a message or a pixel-art card.

GROUP GARDENS
• Add me to a group and type /garden there to start that group's garden. Thanks you tick "share" grow in the garden as plants — one plant per shared thanks, no names.
• Pick your plant when you open a garden; tap any plant to read its thanks.
• See your gardens in the 1:1 app, under the Garden tab — or type /seegardens here.
• /sharerecap in the group posts the group's shared thanks, each with its plant.
• Gardens reset at 3am too.

COMMANDS
• /thanks <note> — log a thanks from chat
• /recap — save today's list in this chat
• /share — today's shareable list
• /garden — start or open a group garden (groups only)
• /sharerecap — the group's shared thanks (groups only)
• /seegardens — your group gardens

No streaks. No pressure. Just one quiet habit.
