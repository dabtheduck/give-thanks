# WELCOME
Hi friend! This little app is just a counter — but a counter with a memory. Press it when something good, small or ordinary happens.


# Why Count Thanks At All?
I went on a day trip to JB with a friend mid-November during annual leave. This was post- biggest- burn- out- ever in my short history of being a working adult. As we were being silly clowns in a Mr DIY shop, I saw a small digital counter. It was shaped like a ring, could fit on a finger, and has one simple job -- to count things. I joked, "Every time someone pisses me off at work I'll press this once." And I bought it. To do that exact thing. It seemed like a brilliant idea at the time...to add some fun to the daily annoyances.

So on my first day back at work after leave, I brought the ring counter with me. There were several instances where I remembered exactly why I wanted to quit my job. Other instances where I could feel my supposedly renewed store of patience plummet immediately into nothingness. "I'll add this to the counter later," I thought. After finally leaving my work place a whole hour late, I had about 3-5 things that had pissed me off recorded loosely via my counter. But as I was pressing the button of the counter to keep track of the unhappiness from the day, I had a sudden realization. 

"Why was I keeping track of the bad? Why not the good?" 

After all, (and here my brain spat out some vague science-sounding phrases) something something neuroplasticity something something what we focus on is what we end up noticing more often. Basically, if I'm always on the look out for things to complain about, my brain zooms in on that and ruminates on that. Then, I'll be somewhat biased and feel like the whole day has been absolutely horrible. What a sad use of brain memory space! 

So then and there, I pressed the reset dot on the counter and tried listing things I was thankful for. The counter went up to 13. It started with 3, then I really itched to press the button more (cos it was so satisfying). So I kept trying to think of more things to be grateful about. And the more I thought about it, the more good I found in my day. If I had a day with 13 good things in it, doesn't that beat the 3 horrible things? Oh wow, suddenly the whole shift felt like a good shift!


# How to Use This Mini App
1:1 with bot chat: 
• log your daily thanks 
• get /recap to save it in your chat with the app 
• share it with others if you want to

Add the bot to group chats:
• start group gardens - your thanks (that you tick "share") will grow in the group garden
• view group gardens from your 1:1 chat with the bot 
• /sharerecap generates a collective recap of thanks grown in the garden  

Important things to note: 
• at 3am every day, your thanks counter resets and the notes will disappear forever unless you save it in your 1:1 bot chat (e.g. /recap command or "send today's recap to chat")
• group gardens also reset at 3am!
