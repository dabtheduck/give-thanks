# About Give Thanks

Give Thanks is a tiny daily habit. Press, notice one good thing, move on.

## Why giving thanks?

In studies of gratitude journaling, people who regularly note things they are grateful for tend to report higher wellbeing than control groups. Effects are modest, and they vary person to person — this is a habit, not a cure.
